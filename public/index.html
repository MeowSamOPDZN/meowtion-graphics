faq

Questions, answered.

 

What is Meowtion Graphics?

An AI motion graphics studio in the browser. Pick a video template, write a short brief, add your brand — and get a short animated brand video for your launch, your website or your social posts.

How long does it take?

Your first version is ready in under a minute — the AI writes the copy and the template renders instantly. Exporting the video file takes a few seconds more.

What exactly do I get?

A short animated video, roughly 9 to 12 seconds depending on the template, in widescreen 16:9 or vertical 9:16. You can download it as a WebM video file.

Can it match my brand?

Yes. Add your brand name, pick your two brand colours and upload your logo. Templates place them wherever the design has room.

What does it cost?

Making videos is free while Meowtion Graphics is in beta. No card, no sign-up.

Can I change the result?

Yes. Edit the headline, subline or call to action directly, tweak your colours, or pick another template and generate again.

Who owns the videos?

You do. There are no watermarks, and you can use your videos anywhere.
